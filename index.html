Tokens

Size, weight, colour and speed are all tokens. Nothing is hard-coded, so the same spinner works at 16px in a button or 64px on an empty state.

Accessibility

Turn on Reduce Motion in your OS and reload: every spinner slows or exchanges motion for a fade rather than freezing. Turn on a high contrast theme and gradient fills become borders. Each example carries role="status" and a visually hidden label.
